ABOUT HIYAB

Bringing Hearts Closer to Eritrea

Hiyab Surprise Deliver is being created to help people send gifts and everyday essentials to family, friends and loved ones in Eritrea.

From birthdays and weddings to engagements, baptisms and holidays, our goal is to make every occasion feel special—even when you are far apart.

Our planned catalogue includes livestock packages, flowers, cakes, bakery items, eggs, fresh vegetables, perfumes and surprise gift baskets.
